# Common Issues and Fixes

## Respond.io Broadcast / Workflow Issue
Check:
1. Template approval status
2. Correct WhatsApp number
3. Customer opt-in / conversation window
4. Whether utility or marketing template is required
5. Whether workflow trigger condition is correct

## Appointment Reminder Not Sent
Check:
1. Appointment date and time
2. Whether customer is SC, PRHB, Promo 8, or another source
3. Reminder timing rule
4. Whether appointment was booked only 1 day before
5. Whether customer rescheduled

## Customer Says No One Contacted Her After Purchase
Internal action:
1. Check order/source/report
2. Check whether client purchased home-based or centre-based
3. Check whether CSO/casesheet/Aoikumo has record
4. Inform relevant team to arrange follow-up
5. Add internal reminder if centre team needs to alert CSO after home-based purchase

## Customer Wants Refund
Internal rule:
- Do not promise refund.
- Ask for reason first.
- Escalate to manager.
- If approved, collect account holder name, bank name, bank account number.
- Update refund record sheet.
- Update CSO Casesheet remark and MRL Refund WhatsApp group.
