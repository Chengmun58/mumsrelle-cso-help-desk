# Medical and Contraindication Policy

## General Rule
If a customer has any medical condition, recent surgery, severe symptoms, implants, pregnancy, fever, infection, or uncertainty, CSO should not give a final medical clearance. Escalate internally and request doctor/gynaecologist clearance where needed.

## Conditions Requiring Caution / Escalation

### Cardiovascular & Vascular
- High blood pressure
- Severe hypertension
- Varicose veins
- Heart disease
- Cardiovascular conditions
- Thrombophlebitis
- Arrhythmia
- Medical edema

### Metabolic
- Diabetes
- Thyroid disorders

### Neurological
- Epilepsy
- Bell's palsy
- Inflamed nerve
- Nervous system dysfunction
- Trapped or pinched nerve, e.g. sciatica
- Regional ganglions

### Dermatological
- Skin conditions
- Scar tissues
- Wounds and burns

### Oncological
- Malignant tumours
- Cancer

### Musculoskeletal
- Arthritis
- Cervical spondylitis
- Acute rheumatism
- Postural deformities
- Osteoporosis
- Slipped disc

### Special Conditions
- Menstruation: Indiba treatment may proceed depending on comfort; PRHB should avoid heavy flow
- Pregnancy
- Nervous or psychotic conditions
- Hernia
- Inflammation
- Currently being treated by GP or complementary practitioner
- Hematoma
- Localised swelling
- Artificial filling in the body
- Undiagnosed lumps and bumps
- Contagious or infectious diseases
- Fibroid: check with gynae first

## Hysterectomy Reference
Customer may usually begin once wounds are healed and with doctor’s clearance, commonly around 6–8 weeks after surgery. For contraindications, customer should check with her gynae.
