# PRHB FAQ — Home-based Pelvic Restoration

## What is Pelvic Restoration Treatment?

Pelvic Restoration Treatment is a gentle palm-based therapy available at home or in-centre. It helps with:
- Reducing urinary incontinence
- Improving pelvic misalignment
- Addressing diastasis recti
- Relieving lower back and hip pain

## When can I start after delivery?

- Natural birth: usually 1 month after childbirth
- C-section: usually 3–6 months, subject to individual condition

If there is uncertainty, request doctor/gynaecologist clearance.

## Is Pelvic Restoration painful?

Customer-facing answer:

Our treatments are designed for your comfort. Some discomfort may be normal, especially if the surrounding muscles are tight or sensitive, but our therapists will adjust according to your comfort level.

## How often should I do it?

Recommended frequency: once every 1–2 weeks, excluding menstruation period.

## What should I prepare?

For PRHB appointment:
- Have a light meal approximately one hour beforehand
- Wear sport attire and light-coloured leggings if possible
- Prepare 2 big, thick towels
- Ensure no fever, pregnancy, or menstruation during appointment

## Contraindications

PRHB is not suitable for clients with:
- Menstrual period, especially heavy or irregular flow
- Past or current fractures in lower back, pelvis, or thigh bone
- Implants in surgical area, such as metal pins
- Fever, acute infection, or contagious disease
- Bone infection, bone tuberculosis, malignant bone tumours
- Injuries or damaged skin at treatment area
- Recent acute spinal cord injury
- Severe abdominal or pelvic pain
- Pregnancy
- Osteoporosis or brittle bone conditions
- Mental illness
