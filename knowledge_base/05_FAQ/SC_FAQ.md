## SC-FAQ-01 — What does SC mean?

**Approved Answer:**  
SC means Slimming Centre.

**Escalation Required:** No

---

## SC-FAQ-02 — Who are SC customers?

**Approved Answer:**  
SC customers are Case Sheet clients whose status is not Decline.

**Escalation Required:** No

---

## SC-FAQ-03 — When are SC reminders sent?

**Approved Answer:**  
SC reminders are sent 2 days and 1 day before appointment.

**Escalation Required:** No

---

## SC-FAQ-04 — What if the appointment is booked only 1 day before?

**Approved Answer:**  
If the appointment is booked only 1 day before, send only the 1-day reminder.

**Escalation Required:** No

---

## SC-FAQ-05 — What if a customer reschedules?

**Approved Answer:**  
If customer reschedules, reminder timing follows the new appointment date.

**Escalation Required:** No
