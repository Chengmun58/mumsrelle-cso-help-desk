## Can I do treatment during menses?

Customer-facing answer:

For Indiba treatment, it is generally safe during menstruation as it does not directly affect the hormonal cycle. However, some clients may experience heavier flow due to improved circulation. If you feel comfortable and do not have discomfort, the treatment may proceed.

For Pelvic Restoration, we usually recommend avoiding treatment during heavy menstrual flow because the session involves manipulation techniques, and menstruation may increase sensitivity, bloating, or cramps.

## Can breastfeeding clients do BA and treatments?

Customer-facing answer:

All machines are breastfeeding-friendly. For ELM and HIFEM treatments, such as Sculpt & Recovery and ELM Pelvic Care, we advise discarding the first pump of breast milk after treatment as a precaution. This is similar to post-exercise care and offers added peace of mind.
