# SOP — SC Appointment Handling

## Scope
This SOP covers Slimming Centre appointment booking, reminder, confirmation, and rescheduling.

## Appointment Duration
A standard SC appointment may include:
- Complimentary full-body assessment: approximately 15–30 minutes
- Treatment: approximately 30–60 minutes, depending on treatment
- Recommended reserved slot: 1–2.5 hours depending on case

## Booking Message — BA / Trial
Customer-facing message:

Hi dear, your appointment is all set. We kindly ask that you arrive on time, as our therapists have back-to-back appointments.

We will also send you a reminder before your appointment. You may reply to confirm your attendance so we can prepare everything ahead for you.

Looking forward to seeing you soon.

## Reminder Rule
SC reminders are sent:
- 2 days before appointment
- 1 day before appointment

If appointment is booked only 1 day before, send only the 1-day reminder.

## Rescheduling Rule
If customer reschedules, reminder timing follows the new appointment date.

## Customer Late / Reschedule Message
If the customer needs to reschedule, ask them to inform at least 2 hours in advance where possible.
