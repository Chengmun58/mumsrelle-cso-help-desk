# Company Overview — Mumsrelle

## Brand Story
Mumsrelle is a wellness centre dedicated to rejuvenation and revitalisation for postpartum mothers. The brand focuses on helping mothers accept and celebrate their transformed selves with confidence and grace.

## Meaning of Mumsrelle
Mumsrelle combines:
- `mums`
- `rejuvenation`
- `belle` — beauty in French

## Vision
To be the premier wellness centre and leading innovator in effective treatments for mothers in Singapore.

## Mission
To create a nurturing and rejuvenating environment for postpartum mothers.
